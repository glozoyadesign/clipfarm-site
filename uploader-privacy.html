Music Uploader Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

What this application is

The Music Uploader is a private, single-purpose publishing tool. It uploads instrumental music videos to one YouTube channel owned by the operator. It is not offered to, or used by, anyone else.

What data we access

Through the YouTube Data API and YouTube Live Streaming API, the app accesses only the operator's own channel. It uploads video files, sets each video's title, description, tags, category, thumbnail, playlists and AI-content disclosure, and runs the channel's own live stream.

What we don't do

The app does not collect any data from viewers, subscribers or commenters. It does not read data from other channels or Google accounts. No data is sold, rented, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Data retention and security

OAuth credentials are stored securely on the operator's own computer and are used only to manage the operator's channel. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions

Compliance

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
