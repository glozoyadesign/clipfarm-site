Clip Farm Automation — Terms of Service

Last updated September 2026

What this is

Clip Farm Automation is a personal content-automation tool operated by its owner. It selects, edits, and publishes short video clips to the owner's own social media accounts (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X). It does not operate on behalf of, or publish to, any account other than the owner's own.

Scope

This tool is not a public service and does not offer accounts, logins, or publishing on behalf of third parties. Any API access it requests (including TikTok's Content Posting API) is used solely to publish content to the owner's own connected TikTok account.

Data

The tool processes publicly available stream/clip content for editing and posting purposes, and platform engagement data (views, comments, likes) for its own connected accounts. It does not collect, sell, or share personal data belonging to viewers or commenters beyond replying to comments on the owner's own posts.

Contact

Questions about this tool or its use of connected platform APIs can be directed to the account owner via the TikTok Developer Portal contact on file for this application.
