Clip Farm Automation — Privacy Policy

Last updated September 2026

Who this covers

Clip Farm Automation is a personal content-automation tool operated by its owner, posting to the owner's own social media accounts only. This policy covers what data the tool touches while doing that.

What is collected

The tool reads publicly available stream/video content for editing and posting, and engagement data (views, comments, likes) on its own connected accounts, including via TikTok's Content Posting API. It does not collect personal data from viewers or commenters, does not track individuals across platforms, and does not sell or share any data with third parties.

Comment replies

The tool may auto-generate short replies to comments left on the owner's own posts. Only the comment text and the reply are processed for this purpose; no other data about the commenter is stored.

Storage

Video files are stored temporarily in a private cloud bucket solely to make them fetchable by each platform's publishing API, and are deleted automatically after a short retention window.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the account owner via the TikTok Developer Portal contact on file for this application.
