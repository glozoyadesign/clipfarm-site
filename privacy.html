Privacy Policy

Effective September 28, 2026 · Applies to the "Unsolved & Declassified" YouTube publishing application

What this application is

This is a single-purpose publishing tool operated by Redacted History Media. It automatically uploads video episodes to one YouTube channel that we own and operate — Unsolved & Declassified (@UnsolvedDeclassified). It is not a public product, and it is not used by, or made available to, any other person or organization.

What data we access

Through the YouTube Data API, this application accesses only the data belonging to our own channel, specifically to:

  • Upload video files to the Unsolved & Declassified channel
  • Set that video's own title, description, tags, category, and AI-content disclosure metadata

It does not read, request, or store data belonging to any other YouTube channel, viewer, or Google account.

What we don't do

  • We do not collect personal data from viewers, subscribers, or commenters.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties.
  • We do not use accessed data for advertising or for any purpose beyond publishing our own channel's content.
  • We do not use this access for any human review of content outside of what is required to operate or debug the application itself.

Data retention & security

OAuth credentials used by this application are stored securely and used solely to authenticate uploads to our own channel. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account security settings of the channel's own Google account. Revoking access immediately stops all further use.

Compliance

This application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
